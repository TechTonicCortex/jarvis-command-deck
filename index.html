How the confirm-tagged commands work — a mishearing can never trigger these on its own:

"Jarvis, shut down" → "Say confirm to proceed" → "Jarvis, confirm" → shuts down

Anything else instead of "confirm" cancels it — and if that "anything else" is a real command, Jarvis runs that instead.

Hand control — webcam, no voice needed

06

A separate program (jarvis_hand.py) that watches your webcam and lets you control windows with your hand — independent of the voice assistant, runs continuously in the background from unlock, same as Jarvis itself. Five gestures, every one a hand shape held steady for a beat (never a fast swipe) — that dwell, not speed, is what tells a deliberate gesture apart from your hand just passing through a shape.

fist, over a window's middle Move. Close into a fist to grab, move your hand, open to drop.
fist, near an edge/corner Resize. Same grab, but near a window's border instead of its middle — drags that edge. Won't shrink below a minimum size.
point (index only), hold Maximize / restore. Hold steady over a window — toggles it. Hold again to flip back.
peace sign (index+middle), hold Minimize. Get it back via the window switcher below (Alt+Tab shows minimized windows too).
three fingers, hold Close. Two-stage — a warning beep, then it closes a beat later (same as clicking its own X). Let go before/at the beep to cancel.
point, at the screen's far left/right edge Switch windows. Like Alt+Tab — works no matter what's under your hand there. Keep holding to keep cycling.

How the close gesture's safety valve works — same "let go to cancel" idea as the voice assistant's shutdown confirmation:

hold three fingers over a window → warning beep → keep holding a beat longer → closes

Letting go at any point before it closes cancels it — nothing happens.

pause hand controlVoice command (see panel 05) — stops tracking your hand without killing the process. "resume hand control" turns it back on.
QWhile the optional preview window is showing (show_preview_window in jarvis_hand_config.json), press Q in that window to quit manually.

Tuning knobs live in jarvis_hand_config.json — fist_threshold / gesture_debounce_frames for grab reliability, resize_margin_px for move-vs-resize, pose_dwell_frames / pose_stability_px for how long and how steady a hold needs to be, close_warn_frames / close_dwell_frames for the close safety valve, and edge_switch_fraction / edge_switch_dwell_frames for the window switcher. See README.md's "Hand Control" section for the full walkthrough — camera feel needs tuning on your own machine, it can't be pre-tuned remotely.

Fully local — unlike voice commands, no video is ever sent anywhere; hand tracking runs entirely on your own machine via OpenCV + MediaPipe.

Email drafting

07

Opens a Gmail draft with recipient/subject/body filled in — it never sends. Recipients come from the contacts list in jarvis_config.json.

Personality & notes

08
Replies are varied on purpose — "Right away, sir," "On it," "As you wish, sir" — instead of one fixed line. Change or remove "sir" via honorific in jarvis_config.json.
Startup greeting changes with time of day: "Good morning, sir" … "Burning the midnight oil, sir?" late at night.
Heads up: "go to sleep" means sleep the PC (with confirmation), not stop listening. Use "stop listening" for that.
